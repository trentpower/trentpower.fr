# Privacy

The published site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, carries no advertising
identifiers, and makes no third-party requests on page load. Browser storage
stays same-origin and is limited to visitor-controlled preferences and the
offline cache; none of it is ever transmitted, and
[`/local/`](https://trentpower.fr/en-au/local/) enumerates every key the
visitor can clear.

This repository contains no visitor data of any kind — there is none to
contain.

The full posture, and how it is enforced at build time, is documented in
[docs/SECURITY-AND-PRIVACY.md](docs/SECURITY-AND-PRIVACY.md). The published
policy lives at [trentpower.fr/en-au/privacy/](https://trentpower.fr/en-au/privacy/)
and [trentpower.fr/fr/confidentialite/](https://trentpower.fr/fr/confidentialite/).
