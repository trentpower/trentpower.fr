This site is static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript.

It is designed to be readable, printable, inspectable and
available offline after first visit.

The public source mirrors are published at /source/.
The signed integrity manifest is published at /integrity.json.
Release archives are published at /integrity/releases/.

No analytics.
No cookies.
No third-party scripts.

Verification
------------
This release follows a standard public archive verification
model.

SHA256SUMS lists the SHA-256 hashes for the release archives.
SHA256SUMS.sig is the detached PGP signature over SHA256SUMS.

The archive files may be checked against SHA256SUMS.
The archive files may also be verified directly with their
detached .sig files.

The live public site manifest is published at /integrity.json.
The manifest signature is published at /integrity.json.sig.
The public signing key is published at /.well-known/pgp-key.asc.

Typography
----------
The live site uses licensed webfonts. Proprietary font files
are not included in this archive. The site remains readable
with fallback fonts. To reproduce the exact typography,
obtain the appropriate font licences and place the licensed
files in /fonts/.
